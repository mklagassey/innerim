# Host: text for difficulty and host events (S19, S20)

Owner: creative director, 2026-11-17. This is text only; the rules are in systems design S19 and S20.
World voice: second person, present tense. Marian is "she". It reports her life only as her body feels it.

## S19 difficulty names

Each setting describes Marian's immune system, which is the real thing that makes the game harder.
Immunosuppression and a vigorous immune response are both real clinical states.

| Setting | Name | One line on the start screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | **Suppressed** | Her immune system is running low. It is easier to slip past, and slower to learn you. |
| Normal | **Typical** | A healthy 58-year-old's defences, doing their job. |
| Hard | **Vigilant** | Her immune system is quick, thorough and unforgiving. Patches are applied by hand. |

## S20 host events

Each event has one world-map announcement, shown before the player leaves the heart, and in-map
lines. Show one in-map line on arrival at each map, rotating with `outside()` like the meal lines.

### A cold
- **World map:** A cold has been going round the office, and now she has it. Her immune system is busy in her nose and throat. Antibodies are up, and so is her temperature.
- **In map:**
  - Tissues, tea, and a sore throat. Her neutrophils have been pulled toward her airways.
  - She is running warm. Every immune cell here is a little faster for it.
  - A sneeze shakes the whole vessel. Antibodies drift thicker in the current.

### Ibuprofen
- **World map:** She took two ibuprofen with breakfast. Prostaglandins are blocked: less fever and less inflammation, and the alarm fades faster. Her sleep will mend a little less tonight.
- **In map:**
  - The ibuprofen is working. The inflammatory signals are faint and slow to spread.
  - Whatever ache she took it for has dulled. The immune alarm dulls with it.

### A run
- **World map:** She has laced up her trainers for the first time in weeks. Blood is pouring toward her muscles, the arteries run fast, and her muscles are burning the sugar in her blood.
- **In map:**
  - Her feet are hitting the pavement. You can feel each stride in the current.
  - Her muscles are taking the sugar. There is less of it left for you.
  - She has found a rhythm. Her heart is fast but steady. Nothing frightens her much at this pace.

### Skipped lunch
- **World map:** A meeting ran over, and lunch is not happening today. The stomach will stay quiet and the blood sugar will stay low until dinner.
- **In map:**
  - It is past one. No lunch is coming. Glucose is thin in the blood.
  - Her stomach growls, empty. The acid stays low.
  - She is getting by on coffee and willpower. The liver is releasing its own stores to keep her going.

### A bad night
- **World map:** She barely slept. The dog was restless, then she was. Her cortisol is up, she will sleep less tonight, and small things will startle her.
- **In map:**
  - She is tired in the way that makes everything too loud. Her heart jumps at little things.
  - It is three in the morning and she is staring at the ceiling. The night's repair work will be short.
  - She is yawning at her desk. Stress hormones are holding her together.
