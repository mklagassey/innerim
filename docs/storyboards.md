# Host: Storyboards

Owner: creative director, 2026-11-17. Shot-by-shot boards for art's cinema renderer. Art owns the
final look. This file gives the intent of each shot, so every frame has a reason to be there.

Each shot lists: **shot** · seconds · **layers** (front to back) · **camera** · **light** ·
**living detail** (the one thing that moves) · **sound** · **out** (how it leaves the shot).
Framing by stage (interludes.md): far = stages 0 to 1, eye = stage 2, near = stage 3+. Where a shot
says "by stage", apply that.

Global: letterbox 2.39:1, soft grain, gentle vignette, warm key with cool fill. One camera move per shot,
5 to 10% over its length, eased in and out. Cross-dissolves of 0.8 s between shots unless marked CUT
(a straight cut is allowed only into a match cut, never a flash). Sound beds in at −18 dB and the
piano motif enters only where marked.

## Chapter cards (4 s each)

Black, the title in thin type, one amber telemetry line. Sound: a single low tone and the link's
faint carrier hiss. The Ch2 card's number settles from 100 to its real value a beat after it appears.
The Ch5 card's strike-through is drawn at handwriting speed, about 1 s.
