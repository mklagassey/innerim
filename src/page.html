Your ship is ten micrometres long and adrift in someone's blood

You are a surgical crew, shrunk and injected. The scan found a tumor seeded through the liver: 20 cells of it, in knots of nine or so. Cut it out. The ship runs on what it can take from the host, and the liver is where the energy is too. Ride the vessels there from the heart, work, feed, and get back. The host is a person with a day of their own: they sleep, they wake, and now and then something frightens them, and their body changes around you when it does. Leaving the liver by its central vein takes 20 energy. Sip glucose from the blood where it glows amber, or tear the mitochondria out of the host's own cells: five energy at once, and every immune cell nearby comes looking.
