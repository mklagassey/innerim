# Host: text for travel by branch (no world-map menu)

Owner: creative director, 2026-11-17. Supports the travel redesign (chapters.md, "Travel between
organs"). World voice: second person, present, physiological. A world line plays the first time the
unit nears a branch mouth on a map; after that, only Shepherd's nav label shows on the assigned one.

## The heart (a short playable chamber)

- First arrival: **The right atrium. Blood from the whole body pours in around you and pauses, for half a beat, before the valve.**
- On the beat: **The tricuspid opens. You go with it.**
- Left side, outbound: **The left ventricle squeezes, and the aorta takes you at a speed nothing else in her body moves.**
- Shepherd label, assigned outflow: **ASSIGNED: AORTA → CELIAC TRUNK → HEPATIC ARTERY**

## Branch mouths (each map's exits, sensed before seen)

| Branch | Sense line (world, first time) | Character for art and audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Celiac trunk | **The aorta splits here. Three ways: one bright, one warm, one dark.** | A three-way fork, a hard pulse |
| Hepatic artery | **Wide and bright, the current pulling hard toward the liver. The escort beacons line it.** | Red, fast, Shepherd's faint dashed lights along the wall |
| Left gastric artery | **Narrow, and warm. The walls ahead have a green tint, and the blood tastes faintly of acid.** | A green glow growing at the far end |
| Splenic artery | **Long and winding, toward a dark red mass where old blood goes to be taken apart.** | Deep maroon, slower (future: the spleen) |
| Superior mesenteric artery | **Dark and busy. The walls are thick with immune cells, and something huge and alive is on the other side of them.** | Dark, crowded with patrol lights, a low hum |
| Portal vein | **Slow and heavy, full of everything the gut just absorbed. It all goes to the liver first.** | Blue-purple, thick, flotsam |
| Hepatic vein | **The liver's drains join here. The blood is cleaner, darker, and in no hurry.** | Blue, calm |
| Cystic vein | **A thin vein off to the side, toward a green-gold sac: the gallbladder.** | Small, yellow-green light |
| Vena cava | **The great vein home. Everything from below the heart is in here with you.** | Wide, slow, blue |
| Pulmonary artery | **Out of the right heart, toward the lungs. The blood is dark and starved of oxygen.** | Dark blue, then a cold white light ahead |
| Cephalic vein (arm) | **Up and out along her arm, just under the skin. You can feel the outside close above you.** | Pale, shallow, a soft daylight through the wall |

## Shepherd's nav labels (amber, on the assigned branch only)

- `ASSIGNED` + the branch name, while Shepherd still orders the unit.
- Off route: the label fades, and Shepherd says: **Unit off assigned route.**
- At stage 4: no labels. The unit names the branches itself, in its serif hand, the first time it
  takes one: *the bright one*, *the warm one*, *the dark one*, *home*, *her arm*.

## The telemetry map (on pause)

Title: **SHEPHERD TELEMETRY · HOST 0001**. At stage 3, the title is **HOST 0001**, struck through, then
**Marian**. Unvisited vessels are drawn as faint, unlabelled lines; visited ones are labelled.

## Flight transitions (loading)

No text. Sound only: the heartbeat, and the flow rising and falling with it. If a transition runs
longer than 4 s, one world line may fade in from this pool:
- **Capillaries. Single file, red cells folding to fit.**
- **The vessel widens. The current slows.**
- **A valve, ahead. You go through on the beat.**
- **Somewhere above, she shifts in her chair. The whole vessel leans.**
